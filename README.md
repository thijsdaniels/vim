# Vim

Vim is a health tracking app focused on caloric intake and output. It allows you to log the food you
eat, as well as the exercise you perform, in order to calculate your deficit or surplus.

To make this a little more convenient, you can register food items with their UPC barcode and create
recipes for food you prepare regularly, as well as register your exercise routines and track your
progress within a routine while you're working out.
